About the practice

An architecture practice devoted to the masjid.

For twenty-five years, our studio has served the Dawoodi Bohra community — designing masjids, jamaatkhanas, and mausoleums under the guidance of jamaat leadership and in the spirit of the Fatimi architectural heritage of Cairo, Yemen, and Gujarat.

Every project begins with the same question: how does this space help the mumineen turn toward their Khaliq? From the mihrab to the wuzu khana, from the men's saf to the women's zenana, every detail is drawn with khidmat in mind.

What we do

End-to-end masjid architecture & build.

One studio, from the first sketch to the day of the iftitah.

Masjid Design

New masjids designed from the ground up — mihrab, minaret, dome, and prayer halls that honor Fatimi tradition while serving today's jamaat.

Jamaatkhana & Community Halls

Multipurpose halls designed around thaal seating, majlis, and sabaq — with kitchens, storage, and flow tuned for jamaat life.

Renovation & Restoration

Careful renovation of existing masjids — expanding capacity, updating wuzu khana and zenana, and restoring historic detail with reverence.

Interiors & Calligraphy

Mihrab detailing, chandeliers, carpeting, Quranic calligraphy, and marble work — coordinated with trusted karigars in India and the Gulf.

Site Selection & Feasibility

Help selecting the right site — qibla orientation, parking, growth capacity, and coordination with local planning authorities.

Construction & Delivery

Turnkey construction management — from breaking ground to iftitah, on time, on budget, and answerable to your jamaat's amanat.

How we work

A quiet, respectful process — from niyyat to iftitah.

  1. 01

    Niyyat & Consultation

    We meet with your jamaat leadership to understand your vision, capacity, budget, and the raza required from Aali Qadr Aqamola TUS.

  2. 02

    Site & Feasibility

    Qibla study, soil survey, capacity modeling, and a first massing that fits the site, the jamaat, and the neighborhood.

  3. 03

    Design & Approvals

    Schematic design, detailed drawings, and coordination with planning authorities — reviewed with jamaat committees at every step.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Trusted contractors, weekly progress reports, and a project manager on the ground protecting your amanat through to handover.

  5. 05

    Iftitah & Aftercare

    We stand with you on the day of the iftitah — and remain available for aftercare, expansion, and future khidmat.
